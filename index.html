Metabones Q666 "Devil's Speed Booster" — 0.5x focal reducer

Discontinued Nikon-F-to-Q adapter with built-in optical reducer. Turns an f/1.8 lens into an effective f/0.9 — genuine shallow depth of field and low-light gain most Q lenses can't touch.
